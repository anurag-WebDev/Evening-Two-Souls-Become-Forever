A little prayer from Pune

You were with me, even from afar

Mi Pune la gelo, tya divshi mi tula khup miss kart hoto. Ase vatat hota, kadhi kadhi bhettoy mi tula? Majha manach lagat navhta. Ani he lakshat hota ki tu sangitlela Modak cha prasad tula hava hota.

Mala tithe jaun khup shant vatla. Ase vatle ki Devanech mala darshan karayla bolavla. Mi mag prasad ghetla ani ghari yayla nighalo.

Tya journey madhye tu majhya sobat hotis, ani mala te khup chan vatla.

Khara tar, ghari yetana jar majhi swatachi car asti, tar mi tevach tula to prasad anun dila asta ani tula bhetlo asto.

The wait was finally over

You are truly a gift from God. ❤️
